# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build something useful together. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to follow these rules.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, including with people who are new to this.
- Assume good faith. Ask before you conclude someone meant harm.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may say no, and that a "no" is about the change, not about you.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or jokes about a person's identity, background or abilities.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Persisting after being asked to stop.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile, [@gurfi1](https://github.com/gurfi1). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block someone from the project for behaviour that breaks these rules.
